- #fleeting are rewritten to #atomic 
	- using spaced repetition command on the fleeting tag as a queue
- #literature notes are linked from new #atomic 

Literature notes are highlights and quotes from books/articles, etc. Tag with #literature/unfiled to know that they are not linked to any atomic note.
