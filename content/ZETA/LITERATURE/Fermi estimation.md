> Fermi estimation is the practice of using quick calculations and rough generalizations to estimate values.

When reading news or facts we often hear lots of numbers and values. Instead of taking them as face-value, try to apply Fermi estimation.

- **Divide into subproblems**. This will help getting a more accurate result as it is very unlikely that we have a consistent bias on a series of unique sub-problems.
- **Focus on the most important parts**. Reducing the variables reduces the complexity and can lead to more accurate solutions
