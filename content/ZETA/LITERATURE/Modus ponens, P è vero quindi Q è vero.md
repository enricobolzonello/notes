Simple rule of inference that states:

> $P$ implies $Q$. $P$ is true, therefore $Q$ must also be true

In symbolic statement:
$$
P\rightarrow Q, P\vdash Q
$$
# See also
- [[Hilbert's program was to formalize all of math, disproven by Gödel]] — Hilbert's program aimed to reduce all of mathematics to mechanical inference rules like this one 
- [[Gödel's incompleteness theorems]] — Gödel showed that even a system grounded in rules like modus ponens cannot prove all truths about itself
