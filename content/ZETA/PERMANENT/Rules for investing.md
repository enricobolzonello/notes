**Fundamental Rule (75/25)**: never have less than 25% or more than 75% in stocks, but still the focus should be on stocks:
- sell them at an approximation of their tangible asset value ($\pm 1/3$ approximation)
Invest in companies with:
- ratio of earning to price (?)
- strong financial position
- prospect of earnings that will be maintained over the years
- [[ROCE]] (Return on Capital Employed) >= average of S&P

> Look for industries that have the least amount of change, and stay in industries where the winner is already declared. In this regard, tech changes too much.

> Invest in business owner's that you are willing to marry family

> As investor you should not forget

> **You cannot hope for better than average results by buying new offerings**

For choosing ETFs:
- [[TER]] should not be above 0.5%
